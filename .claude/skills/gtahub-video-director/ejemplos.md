# EJEMPLOS ARMADOS CON EL MASTER

Cada ejemplo indica la variante, las piezas usadas y el conteo de caracteres
del prompt (medido). Sirven de patrón: para un video nuevo, cambia las piezas,
no el esqueleto.

## E-01 · Cayo Perico, bienvenida — Variante A (single shot I2V) · 1785 caracteres

Piezas: STY-02 · SRC-01 + SRC-03 · PJ-01 · ACT-01 · CAM-03 · LOC-01 + AMB-05/06/02 · LUZ-03 · DLG-01 · CON-01/04 · END-01 · NEG-01.
Entrada: 1 screenshot FiveM de la protagonista en la costa. Kling 3.0, 5–8 s, 9:16 o 16:9, 720p borrador.

```
STYLE: Cinematic gameplay footage captured directly inside GTA V / FiveM. Keep GTA Online-style character anatomy and proportions, game facial topology, hair cards, clothing and tattoo textures as in-game assets, map props, in-engine lighting and shadows. Cinematic camera, game-engine look.

SOURCE: Starting from the provided image as the first frame. Keep the character exactly as shown: face, hair, glasses, makeup, tattoos, outfit and proportions. No redesign. Keep the beach road, time of day and props exactly as in the image.

ACTION: The woman from the source image stands facing camera, shifts her weight, then opens both arms outward in a relaxed welcoming gesture while looking slightly past the lens, then lets the arms settle at her sides with a small nod.

CAMERA: Starts on a medium close-up, pulls back slowly to a wide shot revealing the coast behind her.

ENVIRONMENT: Palm fronds move in a light wind, waves roll onto the beach behind her, a motorcycle passes screen-left to screen-right on the road in the background.

LIGHTING: Orange sunset sky behind her, soft ambient fill from the water, no artificial fill.

DIALOGUE — Spanish (speaker: the white-haired woman, tone: warm, confident):
"Bienvenido a la isla de Cayo Perico."
She says it looking just past the lens, small smile, slight head tilt, then closes her mouth as the arms settle.

CONTINUITY: She stays on the same spot; only her posture and arms move. Same outfit, hairstyle, glasses and tattoos from first frame to last.

ENDING: She holds the pose, small breath, the frame settles on the wide shot.

AVOID: Do not turn the character into a real human. No live action, no photorealistic skin, no Unreal Engine or MetaHuman look, no GTA VI reinterpretation, no studio lighting, no character redesign.
```

## E-02 · Militares, la llamada — Variante B (multishot, 15 s) · 1909 caracteres

Piezas: STY-01 · SRC-04 adaptado · PJ-MIL · ACT-07 + ACT-08 · CAM-14/02/10/06 · LOC-06 · LUZ-05 + LUZ-06 · CON-07 · END-04 · NEG-01 + NEG-02 corto.
Entrada: 1 imagen de los soldados en el sofá del hangar. Kling 3.0 Multishot custom, 4 shots, 16:9.

```
STYLE: High-quality cinematic in-game footage captured inside a modded GTA V / FiveM roleplay server. GTA Online character anatomy, facial topology, hair cards, in-engine materials, lighting and shadows. Not live action.

SOURCE: Keep every soldier as shown in the image: olive fatigues, plate carriers, same faces and head count. Same hangar, same time, same lighting in every shot. No teleporting between shots.

Shot 1 (4s) — ESTABLISHING: Four soldiers slouched on a couch in the hangar watching a TV; a bugle call sounds off-screen; the one nearest the door looks up first. Camera: locked-off wide shot, slight slow push-in.
Shot 2 (4s) — ACTION: The other three turn their heads toward him, then stand one after another, grabbing helmets and rifles from the rack by the wall. Camera: static medium shot with subtle handheld sway, eye level.
Shot 3 (3s) — DETAIL: A hand pulls a vest strap tight, then grabs a rifle sling; boots step past the frame. Camera: static close-up, focus shifts from the strap to the rifle.
Shot 4 (4s) — HERO: Outside the hangar door the four stand in a line, adjust stance, then hold still in formation; the leader raises his chin slightly. Camera: low angle at knee height, slow push-in toward the formation.

ENVIRONMENT: TV glow flickers on the couch, a humvee idles far in the background, dust drifts in the doorway light.
LIGHTING: Warm lamp and TV glow inside; cold fluorescent tubes overhead; dim daylight through the open hangar door for shot 4.
CONTINUITY: Everyone keeps relative positions; nobody swaps places. Movement toward the door is always screen-right. Same gear on the same soldier in every shot.
ENDING: The group holds the formation; the camera completes its push-in and stops.
AVOID: No real humans, no live action, no photorealistic skin, no Unreal or MetaHuman look, no studio lighting. No duplicated characters, no extra limbs, no sudden camera jumps.
```

CONTINUITY: She stays on the same spot; only her right arm and head move. The phone stays in her right hand once it is out. Feet stay planted.

AVOID: Do not turn the character into a real human. No live action, no photorealistic skin, no Unreal Engine or MetaHuman look, no GTA VI reinterpretation, no studio lighting, no character redesign.
