## ACT — Acciones principales (`{{ACTION}}`)

Beats en orden. Pega microacciones (MIC) donde haga falta.

**ACT-01 · bienvenida con brazos abiertos**
> stands facing camera, shifts her weight, then opens both arms outward in a relaxed welcoming gesture while looking slightly past the lens, then lets the arms settle at her sides with a small nod.

**ACT-02 · caminar hacia cámara**
> walks toward camera at a natural pace, each step planting on the ground, arms swinging lightly, glancing once to screen-left at the street, then stops two steps from the lens and looks at it.

**ACT-03 · sacar el teléfono (Social HUB)**
> first her right hand goes to the right cargo pocket, then she pulls out a phone, raises it to chest height, turns the screen slightly toward camera, then holds it steady.

**ACT-04 · bajar del vehículo**
> the driver door opens, she steps out with her left foot first, stands, closes the door with her right hand, then walks around the front of the car toward screen-right.

**ACT-05 · llegada de vehículo**
> the car enters from screen-left, slows with visible suspension dip, tires rolling and gripping the asphalt, then stops with a slight rock forward and settles.

**ACT-06 · aterrizaje de avión**
> the aircraft approaches the runway from the far end, wheels lowered; the main gear touches first with a brief puff of tire smoke and visible strut compression, then the nose gear, the runway rushing beneath as it rolls out and slows.

**ACT-07 · reacción progresiva de grupo (llamada)**
> the soldiers are relaxed on the couch watching TV; the bugle call sounds, first the one nearest the door looks up, then the others turn their heads toward him, then they stand one after another, grab their gear from the rack, and file out through the door.

**ACT-08 · formación heroica**
> the group stands in a line, adjusts stance and gear, then holds still in formation; the central figure raises her chin slightly.

**ACT-09 · persecución a pie**
> runs from screen-left to screen-right along the sidewalk, feet planting, looks back once over the shoulder, vaults the low wall with one hand, keeps running.

**ACT-10 · fiesta / vida nocturna**
> dances loosely to the beat, shoulders and hips moving, raises a drink, turns to say something to the person beside her, laughs.

**ACT-11 · brindis / celebración de comunidad**
> raises a glass toward the group, the others raise theirs a beat later, then everyone drinks and turns back to their conversations.

**ACT-12 · arrastrar / detener a alguien (dirección explícita)**
> the officer holds the suspect by the back of the collar with his right hand; the officer walks forward facing screen-right, the suspect stumbles backward in front of him, both feet on the ground; they cross the frame from screen-left to screen-right.

## CAM — Cámara (`{{CAMERA}}`). Un movimiento dominante por clip.

- CAM-01 **Slow push-in** — Slow push-in from medium shot to medium close-up over the whole clip.
- CAM-02 **Subtle handheld** — Static framing with subtle handheld sway, eye level.
- CAM-03 **Pull-back reveal** — Starts on a medium close-up, pulls back slowly to a wide shot revealing the location behind her.
- CAM-04 **Tracking lateral** — Camera glides alongside the character at walking pace, keeping her frame-left.
- CAM-05 **Follow / action run** — Low follow camera behind the character, slight bounce, keeps distance constant.
- CAM-06 **Low-angle tracking** — Low angle at knee height, tracking forward as she walks toward the lens.
- CAM-07 **Crane-like rise** — Camera rises slowly from ground level to above head height, tilting down to keep the subject centered.
- CAM-08 **Partial orbit** — Slow quarter orbit around the subject, screen-left to screen-right.
- CAM-09 **Dolly backward** — Camera dollies backward at the same speed the subject walks forward, distance constant.
- CAM-10 **Rack focus** — Static camera; focus shifts from the foreground object to the character's face at the 2-second mark.
- CAM-11 **Over-the-shoulder** — Over-the-shoulder behind the character, framing what she looks at.
- CAM-12 **Vehicle tracking** — Camera tracks alongside the car at wheel level, wheels and suspension in frame.
- CAM-13 **Wheel-level runway** — Very low camera at the runway edge, aircraft approaching, gear and touchdown in frame.
- CAM-14 **Wide establishing** — Locked-off wide shot, slight slow push-in.
- CAM-15 **Secuenciado** — Static hold 0–2s, then slow push-in 2–6s, then holds.
